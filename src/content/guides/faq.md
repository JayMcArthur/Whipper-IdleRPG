## Why did my level return to one?

The adventurer's level resets between adventures. Improving equipment and the other persistent systems helps the next run. See [your first adventures](../getting-started/).

## Do I have to keep the app open?

No. The adventure continues while the app is closed, as described by the official game listing. Check the return time and results when you come back.

## Why is my item different from the wiki?

The catalog shows base definitions. Your copy may have upgrades, corrosion, abilities, rarity or analysis effects. Also compare the game version and selected mode. The wiki's current snapshot is Android 7.3.1/build 142.

## Is the item with the highest power always best?

No single score captures every opponent. Consider abilities, sets, growth, attack type and the actual threat in the target dungeon. See [equipment that works together](../equipment/).

## How do I know what to do next?

Read Supporter Teba's messages and the scenario, then look at the latest battle result. If the newest dungeon is too difficult, use an earlier area to improve the part of your setup that failed.

## Does a listed drop mean it is guaranteed?

No. The wiki shows recorded drop relationships; it does not turn raw probability fields into an unverified percentage or promise a drop on every run. Some special rewards and conditions are handled outside the base monster tables.

## Can I use the wiki without uploading anything?

Yes. Every reference page and guide is public. The manual item-book and build tools store their records in your browser. No game-save upload is part of this wiki release.

## Are the calculators accurate?

They are experimental. Their calculations and combat coverage are not fully validated against 7.3.1. A simulated win is not a promise that a real adventure will clear. See the [tools page](../../tools/) before relying on an output.

## Why are the guides in English after I change language?

The game reference and navigation support ten languages. Authored guide prose is currently English and is labelled as such. The site does not count a translated menu as a fully translated guide.

## How can I report a correction?

Open an issue on the [wiki repository](https://github.com/JayMcArthur/Whipper-IdleRPG/issues). Include the page, game version/platform and what the game shows. Avoid attaching a real player save or personal information.
