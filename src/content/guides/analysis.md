Analysis is part of learning about and improving equipment. Read the current game's equipment and item-book screens together: they describe different parts of your progress.

## Your item versus your item book

An equipped item has its own properties. The item book records progress for an equipment identity. Owning two copies of one weapon does not make them the same individual item.

The wiki's optional Item Book tool is a manual browser-local tracker. It does not read the game automatically or transfer progress back to it. Keep a separate export if you rely on browser-local records.

## Revealed information and later effects

Analysis can reveal information and unlock effects. Later effects may also depend on the equipment category, corrosion or other progress. Read both the analysis level and the item's actual effect description.

The retained [analysis table](../../analysis/) is a community reference for the first 25 levels. It is not a claim that every player's current limit is 25 or that later effects have been fully documented for 7.3.1.

## Permanent boosts and BP

Permanent boosts let you change parts of a build beyond the equipment itself. The amount you can allocate depends on your actual game progress. A build with more points than you have is a theoretical setup, not a recommendation you can use immediately.

Choose boosts to address the next adventure. Damage, growth, survivability and special abilities have different values against different opponents. A fixed spending order cannot account for every inventory and boss mechanic.

## Special materials and rewards

The [materials reference](../../materials/) includes effect descriptions for analysis-related and boss rewards. A catalog effect does not prove that a reward is currently obtainable in your selected mode, or that you have met its unlock conditions.

Use the in-game maximums and previews for your current slot. Do not combine caps or proof effects from different versions into an apparently exact build.

## Ability extraction

Newer versions support extracting item abilities during analysis and bulk ability synthesis. Before confirming an operation, inspect what will be consumed, retained or produced. The [equipment guide](../equipment/) explains how to approach synthesis alongside the rest of the build.
