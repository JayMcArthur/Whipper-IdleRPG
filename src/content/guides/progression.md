The early goal is to learn the adventure loop and follow the story. Later, progress comes from several connected systems rather than one equipment number.

## Early adventures

Begin at [The First Grassy Knoll](../../dungeons/1/) and follow the next available dungeon in the app. The base data lists prerequisite relationships for several early areas; each [dungeon page](../../dungeons/) links the prerequisite when it is recorded.

Use Supporter Teba's messages and the in-game scenario to resolve unlocks. A missing prerequisite in a data table does not mean a late-game dungeon is available to a new player.

If a new area defeats you, return to an earlier one for equipment or materials. Read the last result to decide whether you need more damage, survivability or an answer to a particular ability.

## Build permanent progress

Equipment upgrades and evolution, item analysis, permanent boosts and special rewards all provide ways to improve future attempts. Introduce them as the game unlocks them. Do not spend rare resources merely to follow a fixed BP order copied from an older version.

The [materials reference](../../materials/) explains listed effects. The [analysis guide](../analysis/) separates equipment analysis from the item-book and boost-point systems.

## Miasma

Miasma changes the difficulty and context of adventures. Compare like with like: the same dungeon at different miasma settings is not the same test of a build.

The reference's monster stats are base data. They do not include every miasma, floor, elite or boss adjustment. Use your actual encounter and the app's settings when evaluating a result.

## The Illusory Royal Tomb

The [Royal Tomb](../../dungeons/12/) is a continuing progression area. Its small set of encounter groups in the data is not a complete floor-by-floor map. Special encounters and scaling need additional rules beyond the raw table.

Use a useful depth milestone rather than treating a very large internal floor limit as a practical finishing goal. Older community floor thresholds are observations for particular versions and setups, not guaranteed targets for every player.

## Fallen Junkyard bosses

The [Fallen Junkyard](../../dungeons/13/) and its [bosses](../../bosses/) require attention to special mechanics. Read a boss's notes before committing to one offensive strategy. The opponent may reward a different attack type or punish an ability that works elsewhere.

The wiki retains older phase and strategy notes with a community label. Their exact percentages and thresholds are not independently verified against 7.3.1.

## Random dungeons and codes

The Door to Another World includes code-based dungeon content. The [dungeon reference](../../dungeons/) links the retained community code list. Copy a code exactly; unusual characters are part of some entries.

These codes were collected before the current data capture. Their descriptions are community records, not a guarantee of the same result in every version or mode.

## New modes

Kaiser Mode becomes selectable when starting a new game after it has been unlocked through scenario progress. The game also supports multiple save slots. Check the selected slot and mode when following advice or comparing outcomes. See [modes and newer features](../modes/).
