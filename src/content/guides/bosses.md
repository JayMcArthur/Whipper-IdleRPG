Bosses can change the value of a build. Before replacing all your gear, identify the opponent and read its special rules in the game.

## Start with the encounter

Check the boss, selected dungeon, miasma, mode and any boss level or phase information. Base stats in the [boss reference](../../bosses/) are useful for comparison, but they are not the final encounter's scaled values.

Review the last battle result. Failing to damage an enemy is different from losing to poison, a counterattack or healing. These problems may need different solutions even if the same setup has a high general power score.

## Story and Royal Tomb bosses

The reference includes the recorded identities and base data for the story bosses, Anubis and Necronomicon. Their special placement and generated/scaling encounters are not fully described by the fixed dungeon tables alone.

Follow scenario messages to understand unlocks and conditions. A boss being present in the wiki does not mean a new player should challenge it immediately.

## Fallen Junkyard

Older community notes describe distinct mechanics for Adam, Panoptes, Nine-Tails and Yamata no Orochi. Those notes are retained on their individual pages and explicitly separated from the captured base data.

- **Adam:** read the notes on changing phases and critical hits before assuming a crit-focused build works throughout the fight.
- **Panoptes:** check its opening-turn and attack-type rules instead of assuming First Strike solves the encounter.
- **Nine-Tails:** consider the cost of a long fight when poison is involved.
- **Yamata no Orochi:** healing, poison and the Magatama interaction make a simple attack/defense comparison incomplete.

Treat these as questions to check in the current app, not a verified numerical recipe. The exact probabilities, breakpoints and suggested equipment in older notes have not been independently established for 7.3.1.

## Make a useful experiment

Keep most of the build fixed and change the part that addresses the observed threat. Record what improved and what still failed. Repeated outcomes are more informative than one unusually lucky clear.

The existing fight simulator does not implement every boss interaction. Use it as an experiment, not as proof that a boss-ready build has been found.
