Whipper is an idle RPG. You prepare an adventurer, send them into a dungeon, and come back to see what happened. The adventure continues while the app is closed. Your most useful decisions happen before departure and after the return.

**Your adventurer returns to level one between adventures. Your equipment is how you build lasting strength.** A lower starting level after returning is part of the game, not lost progress.

## 1. Equip your adventurer

Check the weapon, armor and ring slots before departing. Start with equipment you own; an empty slot is a simpler problem to fix than a perfect endgame build.

- **Weapon:** compare its power and any STR or other stat bonuses.
- **Armor or shield:** compare defense and the other stats it provides.
- **Ring:** read its abilities and look for a useful match with your equipment.

The [equipment guide](../equipment/) explains upgrades, evolution, sets and abilities. The [weapon](../../weapons/), [armor](../../armor/) and [ring](../../rings/) references show each item's base data. Listed base stats do not include your particular item's upgrades, corrosion, abilities or progression bonuses.

## 2. Start with a manageable dungeon

Follow the early story and the advice from Supporter Teba. Begin with [the first dungeon](../../dungeons/1/) and move forward as the game opens new areas. You do not need a late-game item or a completed build calculator to start.

Read the expected return time in the app. The wiki's dungeon tables are a reference to the base data; special events, bosses and later modes can change how an adventure plays out.

## 3. Let the adventure run

You can close the app while the expedition progresses. Check the return information rather than assuming a longer absence always means a better result. Some dungeons and later systems have their own rules.

## 4. Read the result before changing everything

When the adventurer returns, look at:

1. **Where the run ended.** Was it a particular enemy or the end of the dungeon?
2. **How the fight failed.** Did you run out of HP quickly, struggle to damage the enemy, or lose to a special ability?
3. **What you brought back.** Compare the loot with what you already use. Keep equipment that improves the next attempt or adds a useful ability.

An item with a larger number is not automatically better in every fight. Attack type, sets, abilities and the target can matter. Change one important part of the setup and compare the next result.

## 5. Upgrade, evolve and try again

Use the game's equipment screen to see the cost and effect of an upgrade before spending materials. Some equipment evolves into another item as it is upgraded; the reference links the base evolution chain where the game data lists one.

If you get stuck, improving current equipment or retrying an earlier dungeon can be more practical than repeatedly failing at the newest one. Read unfamiliar [material effects](../../materials/) before using rare resources.

## 6. Learn the next system when you reach it

You will encounter analysis, permanent boosts, synthesis, miasma and special dungeons as you progress. You do not have to optimize all of them on the first day.

- Learn [what the stats mean](../stats/) when a battle result is confusing.
- Read [analysis and permanent progress](../analysis/) when those screens become available.
- Use the [progression guide](../progression/) to choose a later goal.
- Check [newer modes and synthesis](../modes/) when the game introduces them.

## A useful habit

Before leaving: **equip → choose a goal → check the setup**. After returning: **read the result → compare the loot → make one useful improvement**.

The wiki works without an account or an uploaded save. The optional [tools](../../tools/) can help keep notes and build ideas; the game remains the authority for whether a setup succeeds.
